What's the difference?

You might have noticed when shopping for motors for your bot that some are called brushed, while others are called brushless. These are two very different types of motors, and knowing the distinction is important! 

Here's how they work:

Both types of motors use a set of electromagnets pushing against permanent magnets to create rotation.

Brushed motor relies on a set of physical brushes to act as mechancial relays as the motor turns. When a motor turns a certain amount, it will flip the polarity of the internal electromagnets, pushing the internal axle(or commutator) away from the previous magnet and closer to the next one, which has the opposite polarity. It does this thousands of times a minute to create consistent rotation.

Brushless motors usually do the opposite; instead of relying on a mechanical relay to create rotation, it uses an external motor controller to switch the polarity of each of the phases.

Brushed motors are generally cheaper, but less energy effecient and can be prone to smoking when stalling. They're a great choice in smaller bots for drive. Brushless motors are generally considerably more expensive, but wear less over time and are much faster as the ESC can control the speed more precisely.
