This tutorial will walk you through from start to finish on how to design your first competitive robot fighter! 

The combat community generally agrees that Plastic Antweight is the best place to start for new builders, so this tutorial will be centered around that; however, if you are insistent on building in another class, most of the principles taught here will transfer over to other weight classes, so feel free to follow along anyways.

#### What you need
##### (No)Experience
For this tutorial, you need no previous experience, just some determination! We will teach you the rest. 
##### Tools
There are some tools that you will need either to own or have access to. 
- 3D Printer
    You can get away with using a friend's or going to the local library for your first bot. If you enjoy your first bot enough, consider getting a cheap printer to make things easier.
- Soldering Iron/Solder
    We will teach you how to solder, however you'll need access to a decent soldering station and solder.
- Misc Hand Tools
    - Flush Cutters
    - M3 Driver(Allen key, screwdriver, etc)
    - C-clamp(Weapon lock)
