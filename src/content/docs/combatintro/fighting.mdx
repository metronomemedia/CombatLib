Fighting can be broken up into two categories; how *fighting* works and how *competition* works.
## Fighting Format
Fighting is between two robots for a duration of 3 minutes. A match can be won in a couple ways!
1. Traditional Knockout
    a robot does not show translational movement for more than 10 seconds.
2. Battery Knockout
    a robot exposes their battery before the end of the match.
3. Tap-Out
    a competitor yields the fight by tapping the glass.
4. Judge's Decision
    if none of these conditions are met by the end of the match, a panel of judges score each robot's performance. The better scored robot wins.

<Aside title="Check Your Local Comp!">Some competitions have modifications to the standard SPARC ruleset; for instance, NHRL does not stop matches for battery fires, and some events have an extra win condition through arena hazards like pits. Mileage may vary! </Aside>

## Competition Format
Competitions are held nationally very frequently! Generally, if you're within an hour of a major city, there is a competition driveable every month or two for the common weight classes. 
Competitors will register their robots online prior to the competition, show up and sign in, set up their pit, pass the failsafe check, and then go straight into the bracket!

Brackets are commonly **Modified Double Elimination**. Each robot is given 2 strikes before being knocked out of the tournament. The more fights you win, the higher you rank, with event results submitted to the national rankings and potentially earning you a spot at the national championship!
